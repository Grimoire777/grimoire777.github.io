Grizmo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Grizmo is a match-3 puzzle game published by Melhem Studios LLC (developer name on Google Play: Grimoire777). This policy describes what information the game handles. The short version: almost none, and none of it leaves your device.

Information we collect

Grizmo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The game requires no account, no sign-in, and no internet connection to play.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — completed levels, stars, lives, and settings such as sound on or off — is saved only in the app's private storage on your own device. The game also keeps a local log of level attempts (which level was started, won, or lost) used solely to improve level difficulty; this log stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Permissions

Grizmo requests the vibration permission, used only for gameplay haptic feedback. It requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal data.

Children

Grizmo is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Advertising and purchases

The current version of Grizmo contains no third-party advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future update introduces either, this policy will be updated first, and the update will describe exactly what changes before it takes effect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on Grizmo's Google Play listing.
