The story

A little robot and an untidy coast

A harbour robot wakes up half-buried in the sand, missing his memory and bothered by one thing above all: the shells are out of order. Up the coast, broken robots are waiting — and someone is draining the Aether Engine.

Match by the sea

Swap seashells to make lines and squares. Four in a row makes a stripe, five makes the octopus — and swapping two specials together makes something bigger. Fish dart out of every blast.

Rescue the robots

A ferry-bot who lost her cargo. A frozen tide-mill keeper. A forge guardian who'd rather not fight you. Each chapter's puzzles are their story.

A coast that fights back

Ice that pins shells. A green tide that creeps while you're busy. Rust that corrodes what you ignore. Cipher shells that change colour when you look away. Chained charges that go off in one spark.

Honest puzzling

No timers pushing you. No internet needed — play in a tunnel, on a plane, anywhere. Every level hand-tuned and play-tested.

Handmade, literally

Every shell, wave, robot and sound is drawn and synthesised in code. The whole game is smaller than a single photo on your phone.

And the tide keeps rising

Chapter 20 isn't the end. A signal calls from a cold, uncharted coast — Grizmo's story continues in the next book.
