A little robot and an untidy coast

A harbour robot wakes up half-buried in the sand, missing his memory and bothered by one thing above all: the shells are out of order. Grizmo is a match-3 puzzle adventure set on a hand-drawn coast — 44 levels across 20 story chapters, with more on the way.

What's waiting up the coast

Match by the sea

Swap seashells to make lines and squares. Four in a row makes a stripe, five makes the octopus, and swapping two specials together makes something bigger. Fish dart out of every blast.

Rescue the robots

Broken robots are waiting up the coast — a ferry-bot who lost her cargo, a frozen tide-mill keeper, a forge guardian who'd rather not fight you. Each chapter's puzzles are their story.

A coast that fights back

Ice that pins shells in place. A green tide that creeps while you're busy. Rust that corrodes what you ignore. Cipher shells that change colour when you look away. Chained charges that go off in one spark — and at the heart of it all, the failing Aether Engine.

Honest puzzling

No timers pushing you. No internet needed — play in a tunnel, on a plane, anywhere. Every level hand-tuned and play-tested.

Handmade, literally

Every shell, wave, robot and sound is drawn and synthesised in code. The whole game is smaller than a single photo on your phone.
